WHY IS THIS CRUCIAL

Adolescent substance abuse can impact brain development, education, health and future opportunities.

Research has found that adolescents who use multiple substances have increased risks of future health problems. Compared with non-users, teens using one substance had 1.82 times higher odds of cardiovascular disease risk in adulthood, while those using three substances had 2.68 times higher odds.
